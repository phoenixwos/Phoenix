🆓 What's always free

No login tier needed — every alliance gets these forever: Bear Trap / Foundry / Canyon Trackers, the Beginner's Guide, the Hero Guide, the VP Tracker, and the ratio/capacity calculators. Using them costs nothing and never expires.

🔒 What's premium

A handful of tools have one feature locked behind a subscription — the tool itself is always usable, only the "save" or "deliver" step needs it:

  • Rally Sync — the shareable live countdown link
  • Castle Map / Castle Numbers — saving your layout
  • Hive Designer — saving your Bear Trap design
  • World Map — saving your world plan
  • Discord Alerts — turning on delivery to your server
  • Gift Code Redemption — the whole tool (no free tier here)

Everything else — planning, building, previewing — is free even without a subscription.

⏳ The 7-day trial

1

The first time anyone from your alliance opens a premium tool, a 7-day full-access trial starts automatically — no card, no signup step.

2

Everything above unlocks immediately for your whole alliance, not just the person who triggered it.

3

⏱️ Rally Sync Free to use

Each rally leader enters their march time to the target. The tool works backward from the impact time and tells each one exactly when to send — always keeping the 1️⃣2️⃣3️⃣ landing order intact.

  • Toggle "Set a start time" on if you want exact clock times instead of just gaps between sends.
  • Use "+ Add Rally 3" for triple-rally coordination.
  • Tap "Copy message" to paste the send-time breakdown straight into your alliance chat.

🔗 Live countdown link Premium

Instead of typing times manually, share the countdown link in Discord. It updates live with sound, and anyone can watch it without needing access to Phoenix at all — great for rally leaders who don't have accounts.

🏰 Castle Map View freeSave premium

Paint the shared castle grid zone by zone, one color per alliance, so everyone can see who holds what at a glance. Build and edit freely — saving your layout for next time is the part that needs a subscription.

🔢 Castle Numbers View freeSave premium

Same castle grid, but using the mathematical cell-numbering system instead of colors — useful for rally-leader cell assignments. Same rule: building is free, saving needs a subscription.

🐝 Hive Designer View freeSave premium

Plan Bear Trap city placement in rings around the two traps, color-coded by proximity. Design as many layouts as you want for free — saving one to come back to later is the premium part.

🗺️ World Map Explore freeSave premium

The full state map with real terrain, facility markers, and banner routing between resource points. Explore and plan freely — saving a world plan for your alliance needs a subscription.

🎁 Gift Code Redemption Premium

Paste a gift code once and the tool walks your entire roster's Governor IDs through redemption one by one — you just type each captcha as it appears, the rest is automatic. This is the one tool with no free tier, since the whole point is saving you from typing every ID by hand.

1

Make sure your roster's Governor IDs are filled in first — Admin → Members. No ID, no redemption for that player.

2

Paste the code, tap Start, and work through the captchas as they come up.

3

The summary at the end shows who succeeded, who was already redeemed, and who failed so you can retry just those.

📣 Discord Alerts Configure freeDelivery premium

Connects your alliance's Discord server so recurring events, shield reminders, and one-off custom alerts post automatically — nobody has to remember to check Phoenix.

1

In Discord: Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy URL.

2

Paste it into the Discord Alerts tool and save. Saving/activating delivery is the premium step — everything up to that point is free to set up and preview.

3

Once active: recurring event reminders, a shield-up hourly reminder you can toggle yourself, custom one-off messages, and Fort/Stronghold rally calls all post to that channel automatically.

🐻 Bear Trap / Foundry / Canyon Trackers Always free

Attendance tracking for all three events — who showed up, who requested but no-showed, ranked automatically. Export the full history to Excel any time, or hand a leader a blank template to fill in and import back — no Phoenix account needed for whoever fills it out.

📅 VP Tracker Always free

A 48-slot daily schedule for VP farm duty, so your alliance's coverage doesn't have gaps.

🦸 Hero Guide & 🔰 Beginner's Guide Always free

Hero skill numbers pulled from real screenshots, gear priority by troop type, and the fundamentals every new player needs — furnace focus, construction speed stacking, Chief Orders, and SvS savings.
