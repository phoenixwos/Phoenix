🎖️ Hero Gear Calculator

Type your hero's name, pick their troop type, enter each piece's current level, and get the exact costs — in the right order.

How to use this:
1️⃣ Type your hero's name, pick their troop type.
2️⃣ Pick the tab matching your gear's current stage.
3️⃣ Enter each piece's current level.
4️⃣ Set your target level, tap Calculate.
5️⃣ Follow the order below — Tier 1 first, then Tier 2.

This calculator is free for everyone, no account needed.
